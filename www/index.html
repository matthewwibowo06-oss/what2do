About & privacy

No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your name, player id and favorite character are saved only on your phone.

Rooms: when you play with friends, your nickname and votes are kept on our server only for that room. A room and everything in it is deleted after a day with no activity.

Movies: “What’s on” uses the country your connection comes from (never your exact location) to show local releases. Movie data comes from TMDB. This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB.
